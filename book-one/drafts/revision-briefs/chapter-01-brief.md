## D1 — Strip Maren's superlative (residual violation)

**Where:** chapter-01.md:273 (Maren's two sentences inside the dinner scene).

**Problem:** D1 requires that Maren's exceptionalism be moral and circumstantial (she works hard, she carries weight no one else carries), never a professional ranking. "Highest in the cohort" and "cleanest diagnostic work the team produced all quarter" are exactly the banned framing. She is the book's normal-anchor; the ranking fights what she is.

**Instruction:** Replace the ranking sentences with effort/diligence framing. Keep the same beat shape: she mentions it lightly, too lightly, having already decided it is minor; the table praises briefly; the conversation swings back to Ash; she lets it go; nobody notices. Only the *content* of what she says changes. The new line should reference the months of work, the survey being finished and holding up, the work being sound, her having carried it to completion. It must contain no comparative, no ranking, no "best/cleanest/highest/most," no supervisor superlative. A supervisor may acknowledge the work is solid or done, never that it is the best or the cleanest. Target: roughly the same length as the cut (two short sentences), in Maren's existing register (understated, deflecting, lightness-over-something-heavier). Do not change her surrounding dialogue, the mother's line, the father's "Survey work. That holds." line, or "Both of us." Those are load-bearing and stay.
