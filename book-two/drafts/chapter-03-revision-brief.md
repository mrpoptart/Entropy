# Revision Brief — Book Two, Chapter 3

## Critical Issues (must fix)

### C1. Ash: "You're hedging." — POV character using Dorenne's diagnostic register
Ash directly confronts Dorenne in words, which the spec explicitly prohibits: "He doesn't push back against Dorenne in words. His resistance is physical." The line is also stylistically Dorenne's — compact diagnostic declaratives are her mode. It reads as Dorenne turned back on herself.
**Fix:** Cut the line. Move the observation into narration. Then a physical tell: "He sat with it. His hand found the cracked leather on the arm of the chair." The recognition stays; the verbal confrontation goes.
*(Voice — Ash)*

### C2. Dorenne: "I want to know..." (×3) — spec violation, "Dorenne never positions preferences as wants"
Ash's "What do you want?" (line 165) prompts Dorenne to respond with "I want to know... I want to know... I want progress reports" — three uses of want-language for a character whose spec says she never says "I want" and never presents her preferences as desires. She tells; she does not bid.
**Fix:** Replace the "I want" framing with institutional requirements: "The arrangement has conditions. The Wellspring does not appear in another district without prior coordination through this office. This project does not become a Council embarrassment for my reconstruction program. Progress reports, regular ones, through proper channels."
Also consider whether Ash's "What do you want?" should be revised — it invites her off-spec response.
*(Voice — Dorenne)*

### C3. "Burnt-sugar note of channeling" — unestablished world property
The sensory detail that channeling leaves a burnt-sugar smell is not in `worldbuilding.md` or `magic-system.md`. If intended as recurring world texture, add a brief note to magic-system.md. If incidental, replace with an established heat/warmth-based sensory detail.
*(World Keeper)*

## Important Suggestions (should address)

### I1. The reckoning paragraph ("She'd done it alone...") — told not shown
The moment Ash genuinely confronts the cost of his absence is narrated in a block rather than dramatized. It arrives without a specific in-scene trigger, reads as scheduled delivery of an insight.
**Fix:** Remove or compress the "She'd done it alone" paragraph. The ceramic cup hitting the desk harder than intended (already in the chapter) + one beat of silence can carry this weight. Trust the image. Let the realization be shorter and less articulate; the reader will complete it.
*(Pace Keeper, Voice Editor)*

### I2. "He heard what she wasn't saying" — three-part list too precise
"...where have you been, why didn't you come sooner, do you know what your absence cost" — three specific charges inferred from a two-word question from one of the most controlled characters in the book. The third item does work that belongs to the reckoning scene.
**Fix:** Cut to two items. Make at least one uncertain ("or maybe that was him, reading himself into her silence"). Stop one item short of certainty.
*(Pace Keeper, Voice — Ash)*

### I3. Ash's proposal pitch uses Dorenne-register vocabulary — unflagged drift
"A proof of concept," "permits... material access... workforce coordination... supply chains... source at this scale" are all institutional vocabulary in Dorenne's register. Per the spec, this borrowing should be audible, either marked by prose or by a character noticing. Currently it reads as competence.
**Specific fixes:**
- "A proof of concept" → "It's a test, I guess. A small one. To see if it holds." (let Dorenne name the institutional term after)
- "Multiple pumps connected to a shared main" → "share the pipe"
- "Permits. Material access... workforce coordination" speech → fragment into Ash's register: hedged, attributed to her advantage, uncertain where he's out of his depth
*(Voice — Ash, Voice — Dorenne)*

### I4. Closing interior paragraph over-explicates the theme
"The managed decline of a city she loved, executed with brilliance and exhaustion and the particular stubbornness..." — the narrator editorializes what the deployment board, triage list, and charts have already shown. The summary weakens what the images earned.
**Fix:** Cut to observation: "He looked at the charts on the wall. The careful colors. The maintenance schedules. The deployment rotations. Years of her life in ink and organizational logic." Stop there.
*(Thematic Compass, Voice Editor)*

### I5. Thread 12 (Identity) needs one dramatized beat of friction
The identity shift ("the plan isn't about him") arrives as interiority summary, not as dramatized resistance. The spec says identity work should show the old reflex firing and being caught.
**Fix:** Add one moment during the Dorenne meeting where the old identity reflex surfaces — the instinct to demonstrate the Wellspring's value, to be the asset, to make himself necessary. He catches it. Chooses differently. That single beat does more for Thread 12 than any summary.
*(Convergence Tracker, Arc Guardian)*

### I6. Negotiation is too clean — no misstep
Ash navigates the entire conversation without a false note. He reads every pause correctly, recovers nothing. One small misstep — reaching for the wrong register (too familiar, or too formal), or pushing on future projects before he's earned it — would make his ultimate success feel earned and the relationship complexity more real.
*(Pace Keeper)*

### I7. Dorenne: "I'm telling you what it is. What you do with that information is your concern." — collapses into Haran
Haran's move is returning ownership to Ash ("how much is left, and what do you want to build with it?"). This line does exactly that. Dorenne would not step back this cleanly; she would step back in a way that still frames the expected action.
**Fix:** "I'm telling you what it is. The question is whether you have a better use for it than I do."
*(Voice — Dorenne)*

### I8. Dorenne's "Of course." opener — entirely absent from chapter
Dorenne speaks throughout the chapter's central scene but never uses her signature opener once. The spec says it concedes and reframes in the same beat, demonstrating anticipatory competence. A natural placement: before her enumeration of the district's failing systems ("Of course. The water main is temporary. The heating lattices are beyond sustainable maintenance.").
*(Voice — Dorenne)*

### I9. Political landscape underestablished — outline flagged this as a Ch3 requirement
Dorenne mentions opponents using Ash's absence, but no names, no specific Council dynamics. The outline says Ch3 should establish the political landscape. Currently functional but thin.
**Fix:** Add one name or one specific detail — a faction, a Council vote, a comment she absorbed — to give the political texture weight.
*(Continuity Tracker)*

### I10. Map entry: Thread 14 marked dormant for Ch3 but the chapter actively advances it
The "catalytic expenditure" concept is the chapter's load-bearing idea. Update the convergence map to mark Ch3 with 📈 for Thread 14 (Stewardship), or future revision agents may mistakenly trim this material.
*(Convergence Tracker)*
