## Best Passages

### Carver

**1. Lines 59-73 (Edrin's letter)**

> *The wheel went last week. I'm writing from Sennet's house because the lamp at mine won't take a flame from a channeler anymore and I'm not yet good enough at striker and tinder to bother in the dark. Sennet has a striker. Sennet has bread.*
>
> *Tovan died on the seventh...*
> *I want you to know it because I think the city does not know it. I think the city thinks dimming is a thing that happens slow. / It does not happen slow. It happens slow and then it happens at once.*

Single strongest piece of prose across all three drafts. "Sennet has a striker. Sennet has bread." delivers a community's social reorganization in eight words.

**2. Lines 527-535 (the recognition)**

> The work was ordinary. / He had been, for a year and a half, looking for a thing that was not ordinary. ... The answer had been in Haran's chest. / The chest had been at the back of the workshop the whole time he had lived here.

Delivers the "ahead of him all along" beat at the scene level. "The answer had been in Haran's chest" is physical and true. "He let his breath out" honors the style guide.

**3. Lines 943-957 (the catalyst figure)**

> The metal had not been consumed. / The reaction had been the reaction of the things it acted on. / He looked at the chest.

Arrives from below (from Haran's prior metaphor), is not labeled, and the prose does not explain what the reader has just understood.

### Hearthkeeper

**1. Lines 229-239 (Ash's response to letters diverging from expected relief)**

> He had expected, when the box began to fill, a thing that would come up under his ribs the way relief had come up at the forge... He recognized the work. / He also recognized something he had not, in any month of the year, let himself look at.

Handles the gap between expected and actual emotional response by naming the expectation honestly and then refusing to deliver it. Two-step reversal at its cleanest.

**2. Lines 137-143 (Esa's letter)**

> *We are not Ardenmere. I know this. We started earlier and we started smaller and we had you here for the three days... If the working is of use to anyone, send it on.*

Most self-aware letter-voice in any draft. Names the asymmetry between communities without self-congratulation.

**3. Lines 469-489 (the catalyst figure ending)**

> The thing was on the bench. / It was on the bench in eight hands, in canvas and timber and tar and leather...

"Eight hands, in canvas and timber and tar and leather" is concrete and varied. Places the thematic arrival in physical objects rather than in a statement from the narrator.

---

### Lyricist

**1. Lines 197-209 (the second and third Ardenmere letters)**

> *We sent to Esa at Birchhollow. The man we sent is Tarrin's youngest, who is fifteen... He has gone...*
>
> *We did not, two summers ago, do what Birchhollow did. We were wrong by two years. We have spent the two years on the channel, and the channel is gone, and the two years are gone with it.*

Smartest structural choice in this draft. Three letter-writers, each showing a different stage of the community's arc. The self-indicting "We were wrong by two years" is the most emotionally complete Ardenmere passage in any draft.

**2. Lines 493-494 (the chest-close)**

> It closed the small careful way a chest closed when a man had been holding a thing as a thing he had earned and was, in a count, learning that he had not earned it, that the thing had been there before him, that the earning had been other people's, in years he had not been counting, and he sat with the closing...

The closing chest (rather than opening) is the right choice for this beat. Humility, not vindication.

**3. Lines 371-378 (Ryn reading the letters from a distance)**

> She read the way Ryn read, which was at distance, with her hands at her sides and her head a finger tilted to the side and her eyes moving across the lines without commitment.

Best physical rendering of Ryn's character in any draft. Stopping at the cedar pipe letter for a longer count tells the reader about Ryn's attentiveness without naming it.

## Weakest Passages

### Carver

**1. Lines 259-265 (the "morning went into reading" section)**
Letter-reading arc collapsed into a single morning rather than the outline's multi-day accumulation. Letters arrive at roughly equal weight with roughly equal prose texture -- accumulative rather than escalating.

**2. Lines 519-534 (the "he had been looking for it" anaphora)**
Runs four iterations over fifteen lines then adds three more. Not earned repetitions but inventory repetitions. Softens a beat that should land harder.

**3. Lines 791-800 (the Maren note interpretation)**
"She meant: this is what is coming. / She meant: this is what is coming for me." -- narrator explaining what the reader already understood. Cut both lines.

### Hearthkeeper

**1. Lines 375-388 (the Ryn dialogue)**
Ryn summarizes letters Ash has already read back to Ash. Ryn's pivot line arrives in a scene that has not earned it through prior accumulation. Ryn is warmer here than her spec.

**2. Lines 527-528 (Haran's long speech)**
68-word sentence. Haran does not give 68-word speeches. This is Ash's thematic register placed in Haran's mouth.

**3. Lines 615-618 (the forge closing)**
"The forge sang." -- lyrical closure on a minor moment. "Was the workshop it had been on the first day and would be tomorrow" -- narrator-side consolation. No Trailing-Reframe Rule violation.

### Lyricist

**1. Lines 529-532 (the "smaller man" beat)**
"The smaller man he was at the end of the eleven days... the arriving had been the arriving, and he was here." -- trailing reframe, narrator grading the protagonist's progress. Mandatory cut per CLAUDE.md.

**2. Line 339 (Ryn's "gently" tag)**
Adverb dialogue tag -- explicitly listed under "What to Avoid" in style guide. Ryn warmer than her spec.

**3. Lines 215-219 (anachronistic self-awareness)**
"Four years ago he had been a boy" -- timeline does not align with established chronology. Risks doing the "ahead of him all along" beat too early.

## Recommended Path

### Primary recommendation: Carver as foundation

Wins on prose quality and character voice accuracy, which are hardest to retrofit. Edrin's letter is the single best piece of writing across all three drafts. Catalyst figure arrives correctly and without over-explanation. Haran's voice is most accurate.

Primary weakness: letter-reading arc compressed into one morning. The outline specifies multi-day accumulation.

### Passages to pull from other drafts

**From Hearthkeeper, lines 127-175 (multi-day structure)**
Replace Carver's section 3 (lines 259-369, single-morning reading) with Hearthkeeper's four-to-five day arrival framework. Slot Carver's letter texts inside that framework.

**From Lyricist, lines 192-209 (three Ardenmere letters)**
Add the second ("We were wrong by two years") and third ("We are remembering") Ardenmere letters alongside Carver's Edrin letter. Gives Ardenmere an arc rather than a snapshot.

**From Lyricist, lines 247-268 (Ryn's physical rendering)**
Add "at distance, with her hands at her sides" as establishing beat when Ryn enters workshop in the Carver scene (insert at Carver line 691).

**From Hearthkeeper, lines 595-617 (letters-on-bench survey)**
Replace Carver lines 511-521 ("The two letters were the chapter" -- tells the reader what the letters mean) with Hearthkeeper's visual layout of letters in rows. Adjust names to Carver's letter-writers.

### Hybrid structure

1. Opening through network reveal: Carver (lines 3-256), intact.
2. Letter arrivals: Hearthkeeper's multi-day framework + Carver's letter prose + Lyricist's second and third Ardenmere letters.
3. Ryn scene: Carver structure and dialogue + Lyricist's physical Ryn rendering at entry. End on exit line, door closing -- no trailing reframe.
4. Letter-survey and recognition: Hearthkeeper's visual bench layout replacing Carver's "two letters were the chapter" + Carver's catalyst sections.
5. Maren's note and close: Carver, with the double-gloss lines cut.

## Revision Notes

**1. (Mandatory) Cut Carver lines 904-907**
"She meant: this is what is coming. / She meant: this is what is coming for me." -- narrator translating what the reader already understood. Cut both.

**2. (Mandatory) Cut Lyricist lines 529-532 ("smaller man" trailing reframe)**
Narrator grading the protagonist's progress. 100% cut per No Trailing-Reframe Rule.

**3. (Mandatory) Verify Maren note signatory**
Lyricist attributes the note to Dorenne ("signed D."). Outline specifies the note is from Maren. This is a character continuity error. If Dorenne-as-writer is intentional, it requires outline alignment. As written, sender is Maren.

**4. (Important) Replace single-morning letter-reading with multi-day structure**
Outline: "fragments, over days, assembling a picture." Carver collapses this into one morning. Use Hearthkeeper's four-to-five day framework.

**5. (Important) Add Lyricist's second and third Ardenmere letters**
Ardenmere needs an arc, not just a snapshot. "We were wrong by two years" and "We are remembering" make the community contrast emotionally complete.

**6. (Important) Cut Carver's anaphora section (lines 519-534) by half**
Four "he had been looking for it" iterations plus three more is one pass too many. The catalyst-recognition section (lines 941-970) should be the single arrival.

**7. (Important) Cut Hearthkeeper's Haran long speech (lines 527-528)**
68-word sentence in Haran's voice. Replace with a short dry observation or let the beat land in silence.

**8. (Polish) Remove adverb tag from Ryn's dialogue in Lyricist**
"Ryn said, gently" -- adverb-heavy dialogue tag, listed under "What to Avoid."
