## Best Passages

### Carver

**1. Lines 282-284 (the relief rendered physically)**

> "It came up under his ribs like a hand pressing a bruise from underneath, the kind of pressure that was not pain but was the absence of a pain he had been carrying so long it had become the shape of him, and the absence was a vertigo, and the vertigo was, for a count, the only thing in the room."

The emotional center of the chapter. Physical sensation first, no emotion label. Free indirect discourse at deep close.

**2. Lines 87-95 (Haran's lighting that doesn't change the air)**

> "The thing he had watched was that the air around Haran's hand had not changed... The expenditure had gone into the tinder. Only into the tinder. A whisper."

Short declaratives culminating in the single-word paragraph "Only into the tinder" deliver the discovery with correct weight.

**3. Lines 505-513 (the two-lines charcoal scene)**

The second line not ending at the edge of the page delivers the implication without stating it. Earns the image by having Ash draw without checking, "the way a man drew a thing he had been drawing in his head for the count of an hour."

---

### Hearthkeeper

**1. Lines 53-55 (two-year discovery)**

> "He had been at this bench for two years. He had not seen it."

Everything before it has established the seeing; the not-seeing is the revelation. White space does real work.

**2. Lines 257-260 (the arithmetic)**

> "He wasted six parts. He probably wasted more than six parts. He had built no discipline. He had built only force."

Clean, sharp, and accurately self-condemning.

**3. Lines 141-143 (the traditions as courtesy)**

> "A man could light his forge and warm his neighbor's house at the same time, and the warming of the neighbor's house was a courtesy... The careful is a thing you find when there isn't enough."

"Courtesy" connects waste of channeling to the social fabric of abundance. "The careful is a thing you find when there isn't enough" is the draft's best single line.

---

### Lyricist

**1. Lines 481-484 (the brightness recursion)**

> "The brightness was the brightness of a thing he was learning, the brightness of a thirty-year discipline at the first hour of the first day, and the brightness was, by every measure, a wasteful brightness, and the brightness was, by every measure, less wasteful than the brightness he had, an hour ago, been making."

Each "by every measure" pivot introduces a new frame rather than repeating the old one.

**2. Lines 337-340 (the not-looking-as-room)**

> "The not-looking-at-Ash was a kindness Ash recognized only because he had been raised by Maren, who not-looked the same way, and had been loved by Ryn, who not-looked the same way, and had been worked by Haran, who not-looked the same way, and the not-looking, in the workshop, was a room a man could stand in."

Chain of observations earns the conclusion. "A room a man could stand in" is the draft's best metaphor.

**3. Lines 509-510 (the want)**

> "the slate behind him on the wall had three lines on it, and the third line was not erased, and the want was still in him, warm, present, the want of a man who had seen a country he could go to and was, for the moment, choosing not to."

Movement from physical slate to interior want is clean.

## Weakest Passages

### Carver

**1. Lines 531-539 (the "if he practiced" chain)**
Seven "if" clauses in sequence. Three beats too long. By "If he stopped building" the point has been made; the intervening conditions about south block, upper terraces, etc. dilute the choice.

**2. Lines 545-548 (named fear)**
"The thing had been a fear. The fear had been of a number coming down to nothing." -- Names the fear, explains the fear, provides the origin. The style guide requires physical response instead.

**3. Line 565 ("The line was beautiful")**
Narrator grading a success beat. Trailing-reframe violation at 50% cut.

### Hearthkeeper

**1. Lines 302-304 (the door-metaphor chain)**
"The door said: keep. The door said: the keeping is good. The keeping is wise... The keeping is the precise, careful, beautiful thing that thirty years of a man's life had bought him." -- 20 lines explaining the temptation rather than dramatizing it. "Beautiful" is a trailing reframe. Most significant style-guide violation in any draft.

**2. Lines 31-35 (summary arithmetic in district section)**
Information already held by the reader. Summary substituting for scene.

**3. Line 21 ("pleased him in a way he had not examined")**
"In a way he had not examined" tells the reader a thing is significant rather than dramatizing the significance.

### Lyricist

**1. Lines 373-388 (the "stay extraordinary" warmth-simile chain)**
Three warmth similes in sequence after the chapter's primary seduction beat. Trailing reframe immediately after what the outline describes as the seduction's most dangerous moment. 100% cut applies.

**2. Lines 361-362 (future-Ash wish-fulfillment vision)**
Fantasy-sequence in narrator voice. Does the reader's imagining for them. The outline specifies the seduction should feel wise to the reader, not that the narration should construct a wish-fulfillment scenario.

**3. Lines 517-518 ("the soft of a thing that was not, yet, gone")**
Narrator glossing the texture of the italicized thought after the fact. The italicized close should stand alone.

## Recommended Path

### Primary recommendation: Carver as foundation

Strongest on scene architecture, voice accuracy, prose quality, and emotional resonance. Failure sequence is correctly proportioned (one brief partial success in four failures). The relief rendered physically at the forge is the best single execution in any draft.

### Passages to pull from other drafts

**From Lyricist, lines 119-149 (forge demonstration, "size of the work")**
Graft Lyricist's "channel at the size of the work" framing as the first half of Haran's explanation in Carver, before "Most channelers push without aiming." Two frames together ("size of the work" + "find the thread") are stronger than either alone.

**From Lyricist, lines 303-313 (three-curve slate visualization)**
Transplant a version of the three-curve slate draw into Carver's coda, replacing or augmenting the bench-paper scene. The third flat line ("forever, if I do nothing") is absent from Carver and is the single most important image for the temptation's shape.

**From Hearthkeeper, lines 141-143 (the traditions as courtesy)**
Replace Carver lines 255-257 with "the warming of the neighbor's house was a courtesy, and the courtesy was the texture of the day." "Courtesy" does thematic work that "people poured" does not.

### Hybrid structure

1. Scene 1 (kettle/arrival): Carver
2. Scene 2 (Haran's forge): Carver + Lyricist's "size of the work" explanation grafted in as first frame
3. Scene 3 (Ash's attempts): Carver's failure sequence + Hearthkeeper's "courtesy" line
4. Scene 4 (alone with the math): Carver's bench-paper scene + Lyricist's three-curve visualization. Keep Carver's physical rendering of the relief; drop Lyricist's warmth-simile chain.
5. Close: Carver's italicized final line. Remove "He looked at the dark forge" if present after it.

## Revision Notes

**1. (Mandatory) Cut the "if he practiced" chain down (lines 531-539)**
Stop at "If he practiced it on himself." Cut the intervening conditions about south block, terraces, lattice, mains, pumps, press, cold flat. Let "building" do its own work.

**2. (Mandatory) Replace lines 545-548 (named fear) with physical rendering**
"The thing had been a fear" through "ordinary again" -- exposition dump. Replace with physical behavior: Ash at the bench, pulse, chest. "Ordinary" can survive if it arrives through action.

**3. (Mandatory) Remove "The line was beautiful" (line 565) and trailing framing**
Lines 565-567 are a trailing reframe after a success beat, prohibited at 50%. Replace with physical observation: Ash looking at the second line without naming it.

**4. (Mandatory) Cut Lyricist lines 373-388 before any grafting**
The warmth-simile chain is a 100% trailing-reframe violation after the seduction's most dangerous moment. Do not carry into assembled draft.

**5. (Important) Add Lyricist's third flat line to Carver's bench-paper/slate coda**
After Ash draws the second line, add a third: the flat line that does not end. This is the temptation's shape made visible. Do not add Lyricist's prose around it; add the image only.

**6. (Important) Confirm final line is the italicized interior thought with no trailing description**
"*He just has to stop building.*" should be the chapter's close with nothing after it. No narrative sentence re-decorates the undecorated close.

**7. (Polish) Cut Hearthkeeper's door-metaphor chain (lines 302-304) if any Hearthkeeper material is grafted**
The door-metaphor passage is the most significant style-guide violation in any draft. Do not carry.
