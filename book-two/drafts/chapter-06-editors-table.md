## Revision Notes

The following are the top revision tasks for the assembly agent working on the composite draft. These are listed in priority order: items 1-4 are mandatory critical-issue fixes; items 5-10 address important suggestions from the brief.

**1. Delete all "older sister" references and recalculate age arithmetic.**
The composite uses Hearthkeeper as foundation, which does not have this error, but any Carver passages pulled in should be checked for "older sister" and "twenty-six years" and revised. Maren is Ash's younger sibling. The shared-years figures should be adjusted to twenty-four or twenty-five.

**2. Cut the Carver farewell reassurance passage (lines 675-683) entirely.**
This passage is not being used in the composite (the Hearthkeeper farewell is the base and is brief-compliant), but if any assembly pass inadvertently reinstates "You'll get it down. You'll find a way. You always have." or the narrator-motivation sentence that follows it, both must be cut. The departure should end at the hug, the thin shoulders, and the Lyricist stair-pause.

**3. Ensure Ash's "I'm fine" (Carver line 45) does not appear in the composite.**
In the apartment arrival, which will use the Carver version, the "I'm fine" exchange must be revised. Replace with a longer, hedging response per the brief's suggestion: something in the mode of "I'm okay, I'm eating fine, there's stew at Petra's most nights, the work's good."

**4. Confirm reservoir math if the thirty-months figure appears in any pulled passage.**
The Carver scaling conversation (lines 649-673) uses the thirty-months figure which does not compute at 3% per month from 39%. If this passage is used in any form, either change "three percent a month" to approximately "one percent a month" (consistent with the outline's 1-2% per week being a high-end estimate) or change the thirty months accordingly. The outline reservoir table shows 1% decline from Ch5 to Ch6, suggesting roughly 0.25% per week, which projects to 156 weeks (approximately 36 months) at that rate. The thirty-months figure is plausible only at this lower rate; the text should specify the rate clearly.

**5. Revise Leska's diagnostic questions to cumulative-forensic mode throughout.**
Per the brief's Important Suggestion 9, the lines "Tell me about the district," "That's not telling me," and "Slow how" are in diagnostic-question mode (Maren's register). In the assembly draft, revise at least the first two to statement-from-network mode. The baker's daughter told me about the pump. I've been building the picture"). Let Ash respond to the partial picture she has, not to an open question.

**6. Add Leska's continuous-narration mode over her working body in the arrival moment.**
The brief identifies the arrival scene as missing Leska's mode entirely. In the Carver arrival sequence being used for the composite, Leska's first actions are holding the door and saying "You're thin." Her forensic attention should be visible in what she is doing as she speaks: her hands should be in motion (setting the kettle, checking the stove, moving plates) and her surveillance should narrate over the work, not pause for it.

**7. Add the combined-picture flicker between the tea scene and the scaling conversation.**
The brief's Important Suggestion 10 identifies a gap: the pump fatigue and the tea hesitation are each filed independently with the same verdict, when the sum should be momentarily more alarming than either piece. Insert one beat after the filing sequence: Ash holds both observations simultaneously for a half-second before consciously setting them into the same drawer. This is the moment that makes "the other drawer" feel genuinely threatened. It should be physically rendered (a pause at the cup, a look at Maren's hands) not narrated.

**8. Ground Haran's "she channels easy" in observable evidence.**
Per Important Suggestion 11, Haran has spent the chapter with his back to the room during the tea scene. Give him one unremarked look during the tea scene: a single moment where the file stills, the head turns a fraction without lifting, and then the file resumes. This converts his observation from authorial intrusion into characteristic Haran attention. The look should take one sentence and have no dialogue.

**9. Confirm the stair-pause (Lyricist lines 571-579) is included in the final composite.**
This is the outline's required third pool signal and no other draft includes it in the tour section. It must appear in the composite after Maren turns and climbs the stair. Without it, the reader does not have the three distinct signals the outline specifies before Chapter 7.

**10. Cut or consolidate the closing coda after "I keep four."**
