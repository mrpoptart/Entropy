# Revision Brief — Chapter 11

## Critical Issues (must fix)

**1. Trailing-reframe violations stacked through the closing forty lines (lines 511-551).**
The chapter ends after a failure beat (Maren's diminishment, the wall pulled out, no help offered or accepted), and the closing sequence stacks roughly six trailing reframes before the chapter ends. Per the post-failure 100% rule, these must be cut:
- Line 513 closing clause: "The way to lose, every day, the small private arguments with a world that had not been built for you, and to lose them quietly, and to walk on." — narrator-side moral framing, "small wisdom" pattern. Cut from "The way to lose" through "walk on," or end the catalogue on a literal item.
- Line 515: "He had built those, none of them on purpose, all of them by living a life in the only way his life was available to be lived." — textbook trailing-reframe wisdom sentence. Cut the second clause; if anything is kept, "He had built those. Maren had not."
- Lines 519-525 closing couplet: "He did not know when the day would be. / He knew he would have it ready." — small-wisdom couplet. Post-failure 100% rule. Cut.
- Line 527: "But it was not a wall anymore." — narrator metaphor reframing the literal exchange. Cut.
- Lines 535-543, the cracked-bowl image's narrator coda: the triadic "the way she had held it after the earthquake, the way she had held it the morning of the move, the way she held everything that had been in her hands long enough to belong to the hands. Her pool was steady. Her hands were steady. The bowl was cracked." — elegiac generalization plus aphoristic chord. Keep the bowl image (Ash sees his mother holding it, thumb at the crack, drinking tea), cut the meaning-making around it.
- Lines 549-551: "The image went with him..." through "He walked home." — three reframes stacked, ending on the same "competence built by deprivation" theme. Cut to "He turned. He went down the street. He walked home." Failure ends flat.

**2. "Three apartments" (line 115) — likely continuity contradiction.**
"she had carried through three apartments without remarking on it" — established history shows only two: the original family apartment (Book One) and the post-earthquake new apartment. Maren's bowl line in the same chapter (line 543, "the morning of the move") is singular: *the* move, not *the moves*. Either change to "two apartments" or add an intermediate move to the family backstory in characters/mother.md and father.md and propagate per the Plot Sync Rule.

**3. Ash's voice is collapsed to Maren's register throughout — chatter-armor never recovers.**
The spec is explicit that Ch11 is the place where post-Wellspring flattening leaks into the family register, AND where the rambly mode "briefly returns when he's defending a story he doesn't believe." The chapter goes the opposite direction: Ash speaks almost entirely in monosyllables ("I'm late." / "Cold." / "Seal." / "Fused." / "Mm." / "Nine." / "Mum will warm it." / "I'm going.") in the warmest room of his life. Even the spec's contrast example ("where Maren says 'I'm fine,' Ash says 'I'm okay, I'm doing good work, I'm helping people, sorry, what was the question?'") is reversed here. Restore at least one or two beats of chatter-armor sprawl, particularly:
- The arrival ("I'm late") needs an apology-spiral.
- The pump exchange ("Seal." / "Fused.") needs Ash to over-explain Haran's verdict instead of compressing further.
- The Davan beat ("He told it the way a man tells a thing to give it to someone. I took it.") — this is Father's placed-brick syntax in Ash's mouth, AND a polished aphoristic two-sentence construction. Replace with Ash deflecting through concrete physical detail (Davan's hands, the bench, the girl's situation, his cut finger).
- "I'm going, Maren." — flat declarative will-assertion. Ash routes around direct assertion. Frame as helpfulness or self-deprecation.

**4. The "stack of small competences" interior catalogue (line 513) is narrator wisdom in Ash's mouth.**
Even as interior, Ash's thought-voice should hold the same evasions as his speech: concrete return, trail-off, deflection. The eight-item anaphoric list ("The way to climb a stair... The way to carry water... The way to find the warmth... The way to test a chair...") is narrator-grade aphorism with the cadence of an epigraph. Per spec: "He doesn't name what his condition cost him. Even at peak grief, he reaches for jokes or concrete tasks." Cut the anaphora list entirely or trim from eight items to three or four of the most specific (cloth at mother's angle, hands on a front, hold a cup and let the cup do what a cup did). Cut the most generic ones (chair-test, floorboard-give). The cataloguing-shape itself is the violation; the items are not the problem in isolation.

## Important Suggestions (should address)
