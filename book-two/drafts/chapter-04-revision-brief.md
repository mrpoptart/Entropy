# Revision Brief — Book Two, Chapter 4

## Critical Issues (must fix)

### C1. Reservoir arithmetic doesn't add up
The chapter opens at "forty-four percent" but the book opens at ~50%, and Ch1-3 have no channeling. Six percent vanishes with no explanation before Ch4 begins. Additionally, the cistern sealing costs "two percent," plus resealing the wrong-diameter pipes costs additional reservoir, yet the chapter closes at only ~41-42% — which is too light given the described work.
**Fix (two parts):**
1. Either add a brief reference to what accounts for the ~50% → ~44% drop before Ch4 (workshop demonstrations, minor expenditures during the survey, etc.), or adjust the Ch4 starting figure to ~48-49%.
2. Either increase the closing estimate ("thirty-eight percent, maybe — thirty-seven") to reflect the double-spend, or explicitly reduce the cistern cost to "maybe half a percent, a fraction."
*(Continuity Tracker)*

### C2. "Molecular level" — breaks in-world voice
"Fusing the stone back to itself at the molecular level" is the author's scientific vocabulary, not in-world language. Characters in this world understand structure through felt sense and craft ("the compressed layers, the mineral grain"), not scientific terminology.
**Fix:** Replace with felt-sense language: "fusing the stone back to itself along the grain" or "the fractures closing the way a smith re-bonds steel, by heat and pressure and the right pressure."
*(World Keeper)*

### C3. Percentage figures in reservoir tracking — prohibited vocabulary
"Forty-four percent when I'd started the week. Maybe forty-two. Maybe forty-one." — characters do not measure their pools in named units per `magic-system.md`. Ash has bodily sense, not a percentage readout.
**Fix:** Replace with comparative/felt language: "Lighter than it had been. He could feel the new boundary, closer than before, not by much, but measurably closer. The cost of the double work was in there, specific, recognizable." No percentages in prose.
*(World Keeper, Continuity Tracker)*

## Important Suggestions (should address)

### I1. Cistern sealing succeeds without a single false start
The chapter frames precision channeling as a new, harder technique — but the seal completes cleanly on the first attempt. The effort is described; the stumble isn't shown. Haran's "better than the Velden job" tells us it succeeded; the reader never watches the technique be hard.
**Fix:** Let one crack close imperfectly on the first pass (stone not fully fused, Ash feels it, re-channels the same spot). One sentence. The cistern still seals. Now the technique is dramatized as difficult, not just described as such.
*(Pace Keeper, Arc Guardian)*

### I2. Tesha's competence is reported, not witnessed
"By the second day she could assemble a pipe junction without help" — told, not shown. She is the most important district worker in Book Two's arc; her authority in later chapters (Ch18) only holds if the reader watched it accumulate.
**Fix:** Replace the summary sentence with a short exchange (4-6 lines). She misreads a fitting or asks why this diameter rather than the one they used higher up. Connect her curiosity to Ash's subsequent lower-feed error — her question is the one he didn't fully think through.
*(Pace Keeper, Convergence Tracker)*

### I3. Dael confrontation ends one beat short of its identity payload
After "I'm telling you it works tomorrow. That's all I've got" and Ash going back to the junction, Thread 12 (Identity) needs one more internal beat: that his position as a man who was powerless and received enormous power makes him a structurally persuasive and personally insulting advertisement for this argument. He needs to feel that gap, not just observe the parallel.
**Fix:** One short interior beat after "He went back to the junction" — honest recognition, not self-pity. Something like: he hears his own answer as Dael must have heard it, and it doesn't land any better from the inside.
*(Convergence Tracker, Arc Guardian)*

### I4. Ash's "He'd worn it himself for twenty-three years, reversed" — too summarizing
This names the parallel directly where the scene dynamics already show it. The draft tells us what to think about the Dael mirror.
**Fix:** Cut the sentence. Let the Dael mirror work through action alone — which it already does.
*(Arc Guardian, Thematic Compass)*

### I5. Haran: "Better than the Velden job" — collapses into Father's backward-looking register
Haran's analogies go forward (what fails, what holds, what's the budget). Naming a past job as a reference point is Father's move.
**Fix:** Replace with a forward-pointing response: "Hm." Haran pulled his hand away. "Hold overnight. We'll test the fill in the morning." Quality implied through absence of criticism. Remove the "From Haran, that was a speech" narrator-gloss, or rewrite it to fit the new reading.
*(Voice — Haran)*

### I6. Haran: "Fix it before you seal it or you'll be fixing it again tomorrow" — direct instruction, reads as foreman
Haran does not tell people what to do; he asks a question or hands over an analogy.
**Fix:** "Fix it first. What happens to the sealant if the joint's angled?" — preserves the corrective intent, lets Ash find the answer.
*(Voice — Haran)*

### I7. Haran: "You calculated for a single run. The branches..." — opens into lecture mode before Ash cuts him off
The fragment "The branches..." begins an explanation. Haran would stop much earlier, before naming the mechanism.
**Fix:** "Single run," he said. He turned the tap off. The trickle stopped. — No "The branches..." The phrase "single run" is the observation; the tap going off is the full stop.
*(Voice — Haran)*

### I8. Thread 2 (Infrastructure) has no view beyond Stonecurve
The thread's definition is systemic and citywide, but the chapter keeps it entirely local. In the convergence zone, Thread 2 escalates to rural collapse; if it's only been at district level, that escalation feels like new information rather than escalation.
**Fix:** When Ash surveys the city from the wall at chapter's end, one line pulling the frame back: a district on a far terrace where something has stopped working, or Haran noting in passing that rural communities have stopped requesting materials. One sentence.
*(Convergence Tracker)*

### I9. Duplicate chapter endings — the wall scene and the walk-home repeat the same emotional ground
The wall scene (lines ~241-263) is a complete chapter close: Ash accounts the reservoir, sees the girl, reflects on permanence. The walk-home (lines ~275-287) restates the same themes with different imagery. Both are good; together they're one too many.
**Fix:** Choose one. Recommend keeping the walk-home's final image ("nobody was channeling, and nobody needed to") and compressing the wall scene — end it after Haran exits and Dael's staying is witnessed, then cut to Ash walking home without the intermediate reservoir-accounting paragraph.
*(Story Architect)*

### I10. Ash's Dorenne-framework borrowing reads seamlessly rather than audibly
"The way Dorenne had taught him to track institutional budgets" — the spec says this drift should be a warning sign Ash notices, not comfortable adoption.
**Fix:** Add a qualifier: "...the way Dorenne had taught him to track institutional budgets, which was not something he'd expected to think of." Or handle in narration: a half-beat of him noticing his own framework and not quite accepting it yet.
*(Voice — Ash)*

## Polish Notes (nice to have)

### P1. Five-item description list in opening rooftop scene
The chapter gives five distinct visual items in quick succession (streets, cracked buildings, cookfire smoke, woman hanging laundry, boy with buckets). Style guide says two or three strong details per new location.
**Fix:** Cut the laundry line. Keep: the cracked buildings, the cookfire smoke, the boy with buckets (narratively load-bearing).
*(Voice Editor)*

### P2. Ash's "None." (responding to Felden) — Dorenne register
One-word bureaucratic close from a character in institutional orbit. Mutes Ash's social texture entirely.
**Fix:** "No, I think we're good." or "I don't think so, no." (Contractions, softer landing, Ash-shaped.)
*(Voice — Ash)*

### P3. Ash: "South face. Down to the first junction." and "Follow the chalk marks." — two consecutive clipped instructions, reads as Haran
**Fix:** "South face. Down to the first junction, should be clear from the chalk marks, I put them this morning." (One qualifying clause restores the social filler that distinguishes Ash from Haran.)

*(Voice — Ash)*

### P9. "The arithmetic of it settled in him. Not comfort. Fact." — too aphoristic
**Fix:** Cut "Not comfort. Fact." End at "Both stay."

### P10. Haran's micro-channeling tell absent — Thread 7 dormant at behavioral level
