## Best Passages

### Carver

**1. Lines 205-207 (the canal image)**

> "The canal ran. It ran thin and slow and it caught the grey light at its edge, and a leaf went past on the surface and went under and did not come up, and a bird he did not see called from the wall on the other side, and the bird called twice and stopped."

The chapter's thematic image in miniature. Earns the style guide's "controlled lyrical peak": concrete, sensory, carries weight without announcing it. Silence as emotional punctuation.

**2. Lines 285-310 (heating-lattice-and-Margit section)**

> "He was supposed to come in the third week of the month. He didn't. Nobody knows why. He'll come when he comes. The lattice is cold by mid-afternoon and Margit... sits with her coat on after the third hour because the lattice runs out and the room runs cold and she's seventy-one and her hands hurt."

The macro decline given a body and a name. Worldbuilding and emotional argument doing the same work simultaneously.

**3. Lines 639-683 (the Tuesday return)**

The forward-cast ("She would say 'hand me the small wrench.' He would hand her the small wrench.") followed by the dramatized present. Structural mirroring of the chapter's opening gesture, now enacted as conscious ritual. No other draft achieves this precision.

---

### Hearthkeeper

**1. Lines 144-151 (trough close-up)**

> "They were standing close enough now that he could see the line of grease at the base of her thumb where she had not, at the basin, washed the whole of her hands, and the small white scar on her cheekbone from the hot wire when she had been nineteen, and the way her eyes, when they were doing the work of looking, did not do anything else."

Three details encoding Ryn's history and character without explanation. The incomplete hand-washing is small and real and not symbolic.

**2. Lines 307-308 (wrist-touch)**

> "She put her hand, briefly, on his wrist. The hand was warm from her pocket and smelled of brass and grease, and the thumb pressed once at the inside of his wrist where the pulse was, and she took the hand away again and put it back in her pocket."

Most physically specific rendering of this touch in any draft. "Where the pulse was" makes it a deliberate choice on Ryn's part.

**3. Lines 429-453 ("Tell me a name")**

The "Tell me about Branner" exchange. Not in the outline, but dramatizes the chapter's central critique (listen first) as a practice Ryn immediately models and requires of Ash. The chapter demonstrating its argument through the scene's form.

---

### Lyricist

**1. Lines 155-165 (squeeze-and-ease bench scene)**

> "She said 'squeeze,' and he squeezed, and she said 'ease,' and he eased, and she said 'squeeze,' and he squeezed, and the assembly went together at the rate it went together, and at the end of it she straightened and pressed the heel of her palm into the small of her back."

The rhythm enacts the thing it describes. Best workshop choreography in any draft.

**2. Lines 406-430 (Ryn's Sirth monologue)**

> "I just bring bread on a Tuesday."

Ryn at her most dimensional: limited reach, deliberate choice within actual capacity, no narrating her own compassion. First-rate characterization of two unnamed people (his wife who will not ask for bread but will eat bread that appeared on the step).

**3. Lines 7-9 (the ceiling crack)**

> "It had been a river when he was nine. It had been a road when he was twelve. It had been a crack when he was sixteen. It was a crack now."

The progression from imaginative use to literal perception traces a loss without naming it. The technique (tracing a thing's transformation through Ash's age-stages) is transplantable even if the specific passage is not.

## Weakest Passages

### Carver

**1. Lines 151-162 (catalogue of things Ash talked about)**
Seven "he talked about" repetitions. Items mentioned once and dropped (cracked bowl, Tesha's question) do not recur. Four or five items would preserve the sense of release without padding.

**2. Lines 503-590 (Maren morning section)**
The coda itself is outline-specified. But the morning-after breakfast section (jam, tea, bread) runs too long. The emotional work is done by the time Maren puts the jam down.

**3. Lines 381-386 (three consecutive interrupted sentences)**
"That's not, " / "It is." / "That's not what, " / "It is, Ash." Three comma-restarts in rapid succession makes the exchange feel mechanical. One or two would achieve the same effect.

### Hearthkeeper

**1. Lines 469-474 ("Don't come down on Thursday")**
"Because Thursday is the day you'd come down to prove to yourself you're coming down." Too analytical and knowing for Ryn. This is behavioral-pattern naming in a therapist's register, not a friend deflecting one.

**2. Lines 373-404 (workshop return)**
"Haran: 'Ryn.' Ash: 'Yes.' Haran: 'Mm.'" is so minimal it reads as parody of restraint rather than restraint itself. The emotional resolution has not fully landed before this scene begins.

**3. Lines 344-353 (double close-up of Ryn's manner of looking)**
Two adjacent passages both describing Ryn watching Ash, using the same technique. The doubling flattens the effect. One should be cut.

### Lyricist

**1. Lines 553-555 (narrator summary of the critique)**
"He breathed in and out, and the breathing was the thing the breathing was, which was a man breathing on a corner with a friend..." Violates both show-don't-tell and the No Trailing-Reframe Rule. After the critique lands, the prose ends on literal action. This sentence must be cut.

**2. Lines 362-377 (Ash's answer and Ryn's grading)**
"Both. I think both." / "That was the right answer. It was the true answer. I'll take it." The outline specifies Ash "sits with" the question. Having Ryn grade the answer as correct violates the No Trailing-Reframe prohibition on narrator-surrogate grading of the protagonist's progress.

**3. Lines 25-30 ("old room / new room" extended metaphor)**
"The Wellspring had taken him out of the room and put him in a different room..." Extended metaphor labeling Ash's emotional state rather than grounding it in physical sensation. Should be replaced with physical behavior.
