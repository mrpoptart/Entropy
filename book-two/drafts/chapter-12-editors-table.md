## Best Passages

### Carver

**1. Lines 5-6 (Haran's wire and broadsheet weight)**

> "Haran had folded it once, the long fold, and set it under the small brass weight that had been a clamp before it had been a weight. The weight was where Haran put things he wanted Ash to read. The fold was where Haran put things he had read twice."
> "He took a length of soft wire from the rack and turned it in his fingers and put it back. He did not always take the wire down. The wire was the thing he took down when he was thinking with his hands and did not yet know what the hands were thinking."

Pure show-don't-tell delivering character history and present-tense interiority simultaneously.

**2. Lines 149-155 (the lacquer ring)**

> "He set it down at the place his cup had gone down at every visit before the break, and he saw, in setting it, that the lacquer had a second ring at his place, smaller than hers, less worn, but there. He had not known there was a ring at his place."

A pure object carries eight months of relationship history and present-tense reckoning. Revision brief: "the chapter's strongest single image."

**3. Lines 91-107 (Haran's three-line farewell)**

> "'Ash.' 'Mm.' 'Whatever she asks for.' 'Yes.' 'Don't decide in the room. Decide on the stair after.' 'All right.' 'And eat something.' 'I will.' He didn't."

"He didn't" is the revision brief's cited perfect dry scene-button.

### Hearthkeeper

**1. Lines 105-116 (Dorenne's samovar held-draw)**

> "She crossed to the samovar and poured two cups without asking... The handle was warm and the body was warm and the heat in his palm was the steady single-temperature heat of a vessel that had been kept at a held draw for an hour, not the climbing heat of a cup that had been freshly warmed."

The held-draw samovar is the best Dorenne worldbuilding beat in any draft. Automatic accommodation rendered through physical sensation.

**2. Lines 487-491 (Davan's survey reading)**

> "'It's good work,' he said. 'It is good work.'"

Ash's recognition that Davan's work is genuinely competent, rendered through the practitioner's eye. "It's good work / It is good work" -- grudging admission, echo without elaboration.

**3. Lines 569-577 (Davan's "I will not accept the braid")**

> "'I will accept the methods are wrong and we will sit at this table and I will hear out your alternative. I will accept the methods are working and we will, at least, be honest about what we are arguing about. I will not accept the two of them braided. The braid is what the Hall does.'"

Sharp Davan voice. "The braid is what the Hall does" is a precise compressed capsule of the political contrast.

### Lyricist

**1. Lines 453-455 (Davan's hands behind his back)**

> "They walked. Davan walked with his hands behind his back, a posture Ash had not seen on him before, a posture that had appeared somewhere in the last month, somewhere in the days between the workbench and the platform. The hands were clasped lightly. The clasp was a clasp a man learned by watching other men clasp their hands."

A new physical habit dramatizes Davan's transformation without naming it. "A clasp a man learned by watching other men" identifies performance-of-authority without the narrator endorsing the judgment.

**2. Lines 815-819 (the parallel-posture observation on the walk home)**

> "He thought about Dorenne standing at the window with her hands clasped at the small of her back, the same clasp Davan had used on the towpath, the two of them standing at opposite ends of a city using the same posture without knowing it."

Lyricist's strongest thematic beat. Both antagonists absorbed the same gestures of authority.

**3. Lines 531-533 (wind at the basin)**

> "It came up the basin the way wind came up dry basins, in a long indifferent push, and the dust at the bed of the basin moved and settled, and the weeds at the wall of the towpath bent and did not break..."

No reframe, no metaphor-certification. The wind is the wind.

## Weakest Passages

### Carver

**1. Lines 215-223 (Ash's refusal speech)**
Anaphora structure is Davan's rhetorical architecture, not Ash's. "The mechanism is the lattice, to them. The mechanism is the thing that has, for forty years..." is Dorenne's institutional anaphora in Ash's mouth. Two cross-character collapses in twelve lines (Critical Issues 2 and 3).

**2. Lines 769-770 (closing inventory)**
"the city beyond the curve had, in one day, written a law and printed a list... and the tooth came clean." Five "X was the X" repetitions plus narrator grading of the day's outcomes. Trailing-reframe violation in its most complete form (Critical Issue 1).

**3. Lines 695-701 (Ash's urgency answer to Tesha)**
Davan's triadic anaphora used a third time in Ash's voice. Same rhetorical shape as the refusal speech. Mandatory rework.

### Hearthkeeper

**1. Lines 159-162 (samovar gloss)**
"The kind of accommodation he had stopped noticing in the second year and had been noticing again, lately" -- over-explains what the physical detail already showed. "Every time he set foot in the Hall" has a plausibility problem given Ash's current non-staff status.

**2. Lines 644-645 (chapter reference visible)**
"since the towpath in Chapter Eight" -- author scaffolding broke through the filter. Strip entirely.

**3. Lines 843-858 (four-paragraph trailing reframe)**
"He slept the way a man slept who had set down a comfort and not yet picked up a tool, which was lightly, and with his hands open" plus "at the pace of a person who had not yet figured out the shape and was, in the absence of figuring, walking." Trailing-reframe violation in two layers. Cut to flat action.

### Lyricist

**1. Lines 557-560 (early concession to Davan)**
Ash explicitly confirms Davan's framing ("The methods are working") before the Tesha encounter. More of a concession than the outline authorizes. Reduces the triangulation Tesha's question needs.

**2. Lines 749-750 (sugar-in-tea simile)**
"dissolved on the way to his mouth like sugar in tea" -- trailing simile. Cut the simile, keep the list of dissolving answers.

**3. Lines 820-829 (closing self-assessment)**
"The no had been technically correct and morally insufficient" -- narrator supplying Ash's moral self-assessment for him. Keep the action catalogue; cut the moral certification. "He had not tried" is the correct flat beat. Stop there.

## Recommended Path

### Primary recommendation: Lyricist as foundation

Lyricist wins narrowly. Beats Carver on emotional resonance, thematic depth, and revision brief adherence. Beats Hearthkeeper on prose quality, scene architecture, and character voice accuracy. Structural decisions are sounder: Tesha scene is outdoors with a lamplighter; Davan conversation happens on the towpath with the dry basin as physical context. Failures are all line-level or scene-level cuts.

Carver has the strongest prose achievement but carries five critical issues mostly unaddressed.

### Passages to pull from other drafts

**From Carver:**
- Lines 5-6 (broadsheet weight and fold) -- chapter opening, replace Lyricist's functional open.
- Lines 31-35, 55-57 (Haran's wire + "How much is left, and what do you want to build with it") -- Haran bench scene, replacing Lyricist's lathe-only render. The "How much is left" line is the outline's gold-standard Haran line.
- Lines 149-155 (the lacquer ring) -- insert at the moment Ash sets his cup down during the refusal.
- Lines 91-107 (Haran's farewell + "He didn't") -- chapter's Haran scene close. "He didn't" must appear in the final chapter.
- Lines 283-287 (the stair payoff) -- Hall exit. Insert as Ash does not decide on the stair.

**From Hearthkeeper:**
- Lines 84-90 (samovar held-draw) -- opening of Dorenne scene, replacing Lyricist's workmanlike tea. Stop at the sensory description; do not include the over-explanation.
- Lines 569-577 ("I will not accept the braid") -- fold into Lyricist's towpath conversation as Davan's response to ambivalence.

### Hybrid structure

- Workshop opening: Carver (broadsheet weight, fold, wire, "How much is left")
- Hall arrival: Lyricist
- Dorenne scene: Hearthkeeper samovar + Carver lacquer ring + Lyricist structural shape
- Streets and market: Lyricist (towpath, broadsheet wall with reading woman, fishmonger, boys with chalk; these three vignettes are stronger)
- Davan scene: Lyricist towpath/basin setting + hands-behind-back observation; Hearthkeeper's "braid" line; Carver for nothing (Carver's Davan scene is weakest setting)
- Tesha scene: Lyricist (outdoors at lamp post; lamplighter arrival is a rhythmic beat others lack)
- Haran close: Carver (lathe scene + "He didn't" button are irreplaceable)

## Revision Notes

**1. (Mandatory) Cut Lyricist's close and replace with flat action**
"He slept the way a man slept who had set down a comfort and not yet picked up a tool, which was lightly, and with his hands open" plus the final three paragraphs ("He went, the way Tesha went..."). End at "He did not sleep for a long time." followed by "In the morning he went to the Cooperage at the seventh." No narrator-side framing of what the going means. Carver's closing inventory (lines 769-770) is a harder violation: remove from "the city beyond the curve" through "the tooth came clean."

**2. (Mandatory) Fix Ash's refusal speech (Carver Critical Issue 2, apply to assembled draft)**
The refusal must not use anaphora. Break into hedged pieces: "Because, I don't, the only thing I have, the thing nobody else has, is, is twenty-three years on the other side of this. And if I stand up there and use that to protect the mechanism, I, I don't have anything left. That's mine. That's what's mine."

**3. (Mandatory) Fix the "mechanism is the lattice" anaphora (Critical Issue 3)**
If any Carver Dorenne-scene dialogue is retained, de-anaphorize the Dorenne-cadence lines in Ash's mouth: "That's the mechanism to them. Forty years of it. That's what they're pointing at."

**4. (Mandatory) Resolve the Mira name collision (Critical Issue 5)**
The cloth-stall/market vendor named Mira conflicts with the Stonecurve crew member Mira from Ch10. Rename the market vendor in the assembled draft. Candidates: Senne, Breck, Idra. Propagate to dialogue and Ch12 outline entry per Character Name Rule.

**5. (Mandatory) Cut the closing self-assessment in Lyricist (lines 820-835)**
Keep the action catalogue. Cut "the no had been technically correct and morally insufficient." Stop at "He had not tried."

**6. (Important) Add the Haran dry aside to the chapter-opening scene (Issue 31)**
Candidate: "Lawyers are thorough when they have time. Always have time, when nobody's losing yet." Between the "Modest" exchange and the wire business.

**7. (Important) Reorder Davan's close so the Nessa non-answer is last (Issue 11)**
In Carver, Nessa question precedes the "I'm not your enemy" exchange. Nessa exchange should walk Ash out of the room.

**8. (Important) Magic mechanism clarity (Issue 18)**
"Contribute a percentage of their daily pool to a coordinated draw" is pool-transfer, which is prohibited. Revise to: "contribute channeled service on a fortnightly schedule, assigned by the framework office to named infrastructure."

**9. (Important) Flag: Tesha's Leveler attendance needs upstream seed (Critical Issue 4)**
Ch12 cannot fix this. Flag for Ch11 revision pass: a moment where Tesha hesitates around a broadsheet, asks an unanswered question, or registers a political event differently. Plot Sync Rule item.

**10. (Polish) Cut "Chapter Eight" chapter reference in Hearthkeeper (line 644-645)**
Author scaffolding visible in prose. Strip entirely.
