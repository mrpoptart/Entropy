# Revision Brief — Book Two, Chapter 1

## Critical Issues (must fix)

### C1. Broadsheet symbol treated as familiar — contradicts B1 Ch22
The circle-and-line Leveler symbol is treated as something Ash already recognizes and passes daily. But B1 Ch22 explicitly establishes he sees the symbol for the first time and "didn't know what it meant. He would learn." Revise Ch1 to show him recognizing it as the symbol he *first* saw three weeks ago at the close of Book One — not as a long-familiar landmark.
*(Continuity Tracker)*

### C2. "Closer to the bottom than it had any right to be" — reservoir mismatch
At ~44%, the reservoir is half-full. Edges are perceptible; it does not feel nearly depleted. The phrase "closer to the bottom than it had any right to be" belongs to a late-book reservoir level, not an Act One opening. Revise to convey finite-and-edged without near-depletion language.
*(Continuity Tracker, Arc Guardian)*

### C3. "Oceanic fullness... gone since three weeks ago" — wrong timeline
The draft implies the oceanic quality faded in the past three weeks post-Dorenne. B1 Ch22 already describes the reservoir as finite and edged at Book One's close. Revise to situate that transition earlier — it was fading through the second half of Book One, not suddenly resolved post-break.
*(Continuity Tracker)*

## Important Suggestions (should address)

### I1. Ash's dialogue is severely underwritten — collapses into Maren/Dorenne register
Ash's lines throughout are short, unhedged declaratives: "This morning." / "I know. I need heavier wire." / "Gate valve. I changed my mind." / "It's not enough." / "I don't have one." These read like Maren's clipped certainty or Dorenne's scope-talk, not Ash's chatter-recovering register. The workshop is precisely where his verbal texture should return. Specific cross-character collapses:
- "Gate valve. I changed my mind." → sounds like Maren
- "Depends on the water pressure and pipe diameter... connect in sequence with a shared main" → sounds like Dorenne (institutional vocabulary)
- "It's not enough." / "I don't have one." → both sound like Haran (flat acknowledgments without deflection)
See Voice Ash section below for line-by-line proposed revisions.
*(Voice — Ash)*

### I2. Haran's long speech ("You've got pieces of something... You will.") — registers as Father
The three-beat reassurance "You've got pieces of something. You don't know what it is yet. That's all right. You will." is validation-as-speech — Father's register (affirming past, predicting future) not Haran's (forward-pointing resource question, silence). Cut to one sentence delivered while looking at the board, or cut entirely and let the narration carry it.
*(Voice — Haran)*

### I3. Haran's four-question inventory — collapses into Dorenne's managerial register
"How many homes... And the heating?... Coal?..." followed by Haran synthesizing the thermal-capture cross-connection is inventorying infrastructure constraints, not handing the problem back. Cut to one question. The thermal-capture synthesis should be shown (rubber disc held near the stove) not stated ("I've felt it from across the room").
*(Voice — Haran)*

### I4. Solo bench scene — reservoir reflection repeated three times
The reservoir sensation is rendered once (lines 43–46), again (lines 51–53), and a third time (lines 69–70) that echoes the second too closely. The third pass dilutes impact. Cut or condense to let the closing chapter pulse at line 285 land harder.
*(Story Architect, Pace Keeper)*

### I5. Two unnamed tinkerers — summary characters creating unmet promises
The ventilation-fan and lamp tinkerers are described by project with no names or voices. Either name one and give them a single dialogue line, or compress the project descriptions to background noise. Currently they occupy space that promises future characters but doesn't deliver them.
*(Pace Keeper, Continuity Tracker)*

### I6. "Twenty homes" delivered as dialogue — not witnessed
The proof that the workshop continued without Ash is reported by Haran. Consider having Ash pass one installed pump on the morning walk — one woman at a crank, one resident who recognizes the mechanism. This makes the proof visceral before Haran states it, and dramatizes the "workshop didn't orbit you" theme.
*(Pace Keeper, Convergence Tracker)*

### I7. Exposition dump on rubber disc properties — cut or compress
Lines 119–120: "A seal material. Flexible, watertight, sourceable from tree sap and sulfur, materials that existed in abundance, that didn't require channeling to produce, that could be made by anyone with a heat source and the knowledge of the process." Ash already handled the disc and heard its origin explained. This summarizes the significance rather than trusting the reader to feel it. Cut from "A seal material..." through "...knowledge of the process." The next line, "This changes the valve design," carries the realization.
*(Voice Editor, Thematic Compass)*

### I8. Thread 7 (Haran's mastery) needs its micro-channeling tell
The chapter establishes Haran's personality and craft but not the specific mastery signal (kettle stays warm, tools positioned before fingers reach them, temperature precisely right). One quiet micro-channeling moment would re-establish this thread for readers coming from Book One and plant the Ch15 payoff.
*(Convergence Tracker)*

### I9. "They're not wrong about the problem" / broadsheet — missing mirror to Ash's identity
The Leveler argument is Thread 3 progressing, but the convergence map cross-links Thread 3 to Thread 12 (Identity Fusion). A half-beat where Ash recognizes something of his own identity structure in the broadsheet — not the politics, but the shape of building an identity entirely around a loss — would make the Ch13 Davan break land harder.
*(Convergence Tracker)*

### I10. Haran's "Language does that" line — slightly aphoristic
Minor drift. This is a general observation about human nature rather than a workbench-grounded remark. The spec says Haran's dry humor is material and throwaway, not philosophical. Can cut it entirely and let silence + tool carry to "What do you think happens if they get what they're asking for?"
*(Voice — Haran)*
