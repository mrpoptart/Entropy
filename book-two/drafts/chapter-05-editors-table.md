## Best Passages

### Carver

**1. The Haran pump repair (lines 241-252)**

> "There. As Haran turned the valve, his thumb passed across the cocked flap, and something happened that wasn't physical. The flap straightened a fraction. The grit, half-fused into the brass seat, released its hold and dropped into Haran's palm. A breath of warmth moved through Haran's skin into the metal and out, so fine and so brief that Ash would not have seen it if he hadn't been looking specifically."

Precise restraint of the fantastic: the channeling is described in plain physical language while simultaneously establishing that it is something invisible to anyone not trained to see it.

**2. Tesha at the pump (lines 265-295)**

> "She stopped. Looked at the seal. Looked at Haran. Haran kept eating his bread."

Characterization in pure behavior. The whole sequence follows the style guide's instruction to make emotion visible through action rather than naming it.

**3. The warm wall (lines 137-143)**

> "He laid his hand on the plaster. Warm. Not from him. He held it there a long time."

Three sentences. "Not from him" carries the entire weight of the catalytic-expenditure theme. Should be protected entirely.

---

### Hearthkeeper

**1. Building Six repair sequence (lines 267-335)**

> "The brass insert turned home under her wrench in a way that worked. Not in a way that was beautiful. Beautiful would come later, or it would not. Working was what the building needed today."

The chapter's best single paragraph of thematic rendering. Says something true about craft and apprenticeship without the narrator leaning forward.

**2. Dael at the end of the repair (lines 319-335)**

> "Dael looked at the housing. He looked at Tesha. He looked, for a moment, at his own hands, the way a man does when he has just discovered that he has hands that did a thing they had not done before. He did not, Ash thought, look defeated. He looked like a person who had run a race for the first time and had finished it, and was now learning, in the breathing-after, the difference between finishing and winning."

Best execution of the "ownership forming" beat the revision brief requires for Dael.

**3. Veth's question answered (lines 383-411)**

> "The sentence had felt true when he said it. It had felt true an hour before Veth arrived, when he had rehearsed it. It felt, now, like the kind of true that needed checking, the way a beam felt true when you put your weight on it the first time. He had not, he understood, put his weight on it yet."

Clearest dramatization of what the revision brief calls "Ash's identity dread connected to the institutional answer."

---

### Lyricist

**1. Haran's repair as felt contrast (lines 183-190)**

> "That was the channeling. That was all of it."

The deliberate paragraph break enacts the smallness of the expenditure. For a chapter whose organizing question is efficiency-gap, this is exactly the rhythm the scene requires.

**2. The tea on the steps (lines 345-421)**

> "The math hasn't been the question." / "Then what is." / "Whether anything works at all."

Haran's articulation of this distinction is cleaner in Lyricist than in either other draft. "Whether one district is possible. We didn't know that until last month. Now we know." establishes the proof-of-concept as a genuine epistemic advance.

**3. The close on the step (lines 433-451)**

> "The work that didn't need him had been built by him. The maintenance might hold without him. The construction wouldn't."

Lyricist's best line. Names the dependency trap more precisely and more plainly than either other draft. Belongs in the final chapter.

## Weakest Passages

### Carver

1. "He filed it" appearing three times (lines 37, 217, 327) -- crosses from motif into verbal tic.
2. Trailing reframe after the warm-wall scene (lines 139-143 extended) -- explanation undercuts the physical silence of the moment.
3. The Felden closing narration (lines 425-432): "He didn't say what comes after the work. He didn't have to." -- confirms what the scene's silence already communicated.

### Hearthkeeper

1. "A district. Not a project. A place where people lived..." (line 515) -- narrator doing thematic summary after the scene has communicated it.
2. Iselle's voice through the chapter -- flat-factual cadence reads as demonstration of precocity rather than observed child.
3. The workshop closing (lines 549-595) -- extends past the emotional landing. After Iselle raises the bread-flag, the scene has closed.

### Lyricist

1. Trailing reframe at chapter close (lines 449-451): "Forty percent. A start. But only a start." -- explicit violation of no-trailing-reframe rule.
2. The Korr liaison named wrongly -- invents Vaska/Talven chain of command; established name is Felden per outline.
3. The reservoir math section is told, not felt (lines 105-122) -- delivers numbers as ledger entries rather than physical experience.

## Critical Issues Across All Drafts (Revision Brief Compliance)

1. **Trailing reframes:** Carver has two; Lyricist has one at close; Hearthkeeper has one. None achieves full compliance.
2. **Ash's identity dread absent from reservoir math:** Hearthkeeper addresses this most directly. Carver keeps them separate. Lyricist keeps them adjacent but makes them explicit as numbers.
3. **Maren's seed absent:** All three drafts fail this entirely. Required for Chapter 6 to function.
4. **Levelers invisible:** All three drafts fail this. No broadsheet reference, overheard comment, or background signal.
5. **Haran's two direct-lesson lines violate his voice spec:** All three drafts contain at least one violation.
6. **"She'd like a report" cross-character collapse:** Carver contains this line. Hearthkeeper and Lyricist do not.

## Recommendation: Hearthkeeper as Foundation

Hearthkeeper is the strongest draft by a meaningful margin.

**Why Hearthkeeper:**
- Only draft that gives Dael the Act One ownership beat the outline requires (Building Six repair with Dael's hands).
- Most complete implementation of the identity-dread requirement (beam-true passage).
- Character named "Veth" should revert to "Felden" per outline continuity throughout.
- Chapter close (workshop bench, drying brass, reservoir held but finite) is better paced than alternatives.

### Passages to pull from other drafts

**From Carver: The warm wall (lines 131-143)**
Replace Hearthkeeper's equivalent ("Something he'd built, that didn't need him...") with Carver's "Warm. Not from him. He held it there a long time."

**From Carver: Tesha's tool-roll exit (lines 297-305)**
"'It's rougher than yours,' Tesha said. To Ash. Not to Haran's back." -- insert at close of Building Six sequence.

**From Lyricist: The dependency line**
"The work that didn't need him had been built by him. The maintenance might hold without him. The construction wouldn't." -- insert into closing wall-sit.

**From Lyricist: Haran on the steps (lines 395-411)**
"Whether anything works at all. Whether one district is possible. We didn't know that until last month. Now we know." -- add to Hearthkeeper's post-Felden exchange alongside "Ready isn't a place, it's a direction."

### Hybrid structure

Follow Hearthkeeper's architecture with:
- Hearthkeeper's grain hall move-in scene

## Revision Notes for Assembly

1. **Plant Maren's seed (non-negotiable).** A single line during Ash's close-of-day walk or workshop bench scene: a remark from Felden that a senior channeler's inspection visit has been rescheduled, or a moment where Ash notices Maren hasn't sent word since the project began.

2. **Plant one Leveler signal.** A broadsheet pinned to the pump platform, a comment from a district worker, or a single sentence from Felden about "some organizing near the Hall, nothing serious."

3. **Cut trailing reframes:**
   - "A district. Not a project..." -- end on the water running.
   - Extended close after Iselle's bread-flag -- end there.
   - Warm-wall narration after "Not from him. He held it there a long time." -- cut all follow-on.

4. **Fix Haran's voice violations.** Replace "You can't train everyone. You train enough. The enough train more." with an oblique mechanism analogy. Do not replace with anaphoric declarative lines.

5. **Trim reservoir tracking from ledger to body.** Replace percentage figures with qualitative description ("lighter than it had been at the start of the week, lighter still than the start of the month, the boundary closer than he could pretend not to feel").
