## Best Passages

### Carver

**1. Lines 3-57 (the rural letter opening)**

> "He read it. The hand was careful, schooled, slow. A woman's hand. She had begun the letter twice. The first beginning was crossed out. *We have done what we could.* The second beginning was the one she had let stand. *I am writing to let you know.*"
>
> "The decline had not collapsed them. The decline had simply outrun them."

The letter rendered as a physical document, not summarized. Two beginnings, with the crossed-out line italicized, is devastating and costs nothing in word count. "Simply outrun them" is the entire chapter's thesis in five words, earned by the preceding setup.

**2. Line 169 (Haran steadies the door)**

> "The door swung. Haran reached out without looking and steadied it before it banged."

Haran's entire character in a single physical action. Automatic, domestic, decades-deep. No tag, no reflection, no gloss. This closes the Tesha scene with a gesture belonging entirely to Haran's voice.

**3. Lines 371-376 (the Dorenne letter interior)**

> "He had built, in the last six months, a small private picture of Dorenne that allowed him to keep working without having to think about her, and the picture had her as a woman who would defend her authority to the last and would, when she lost it, withdraw entirely and become a private grief in a private room. The picture had been useful. The picture had been wrong."

Free indirect discourse at its most exact. Ash's analytical habit turned on Dorenne, with self-correction arriving in Ash's own register. "The picture had been useful. The picture had been wrong." refuses to editorialize.

### Hearthkeeper

**1. Lines 205-213 ("Picture of the system")**

> "They've built the picture of the system."
> ...
> "That's the phrase."
> "Yes."
> "Write that down too."

Hearthkeeper coins the chapter's sharpest diagnostic phrase and gives it to Haran via a two-line exchange. The concept earns its weight as forward continuity. Tag-free, exact.

**2. Lines 497-563 (Ryn closing the scene at the workshop door)**

> "She put her hand on his arm above the elbow, the small pressure of her fingers through the wool of his shirt, three small distinct presses the way a person checked a wall for a beam, and then she took the hand away."

Affection and assessment in the same motion. "Three small distinct presses the way a person checked a wall for a beam" is the best single image either draft produces for this scene.

**3. Lines 15-17 (Haran lighting the forge)**

> "He fed it slowly, the way Haran had taught him, the small careful additions that let a fire decide on itself before you asked anything of it. He did not channel. He had not channelled in this workshop for a thing as small as kindling in months now."

The Ch1 echo done by action rather than by naming. Settled competence rather than practiced concealment. The carry-through to the district workers earns itself without stating it.

### Lyricist

**1. Lines 277-288 (Marren teaching the girl the path of heat)**

> "Marren did not correct her. He let her draw it wrong, and he watched her draw it wrong, and when she was done he tapped the place where the wrong began and did not say what was wrong, and she looked at the place, and she looked at the lattice, and after a moment she rubbed the chalk line out with her thumb and drew it again. She drew it right. Marren nodded."

The scaling problem's counter-argument shown in physical action. "Tapped the place where the wrong began and did not say what was wrong" enacts the discipline Ash has had to learn, now embodied by a worker he trained. The best dramatized training scene in any draft.

**2. Lines 359-426 (Berre's kitchen scene)**

> "He did not say *and you'll see what's wrong*. He waited. He let her look. The child in the doorway breathed. The cold came down off the upper galleries. Berre looked at the spacing for the count of fourteen and her hand came up to her mouth and she said, 'Oh.'"

"The count of fourteen" prevents generic scene-writing. "The child in the doorway breathed" is perfect ambient intrusion. Ash's discipline of withholding shows character growth through restraint.

**3. Lines 694-757 (the closing walk)**

> "He passed a child on a stoop. The child was eight or nine. The child was reading a book by the light of a small oil lamp set on the step beside her, the light bad, the book held close, the child's face frowning into the page. He went by. The child did not look up. He did not break her in the reading."

"He did not break her in the reading" is small and exact. The city as unconnected particulars, not as symbolic inventory. The walk's best single beat.

## Weakest Passages

### Carver

**1. Lines 583-589 (enumeration before the wall)**
"The question was the right question and it was the question he had been carrying all day in the workshop and on the walk to Brackmoor and in the message from Dorenne and in Ryn's face at the door and in Haran's hand on the gear and in the rural letter weighted under the punch." Does the reader's work. The chapter has dramatized all these moments; naming them is a loss of trust.

**2. Lines 517-519 (the fear-history interior)**
"The fear had not gone away in the last year. It had not been argued with. It had not been beaten. It was simply still there." Three sentences explaining what the fear has not done. The outline wants the fear held beside the willingness with neither winning; only "It was simply still there, breathing" is needed.

**3. Lines 260-268 (Ryn's father story close)**
"My father had it too. It wasn't a good year." The "wasn't a good year" softens what should be left blunt. The story works better ending at "My father had it too."

### Hearthkeeper

**1. Lines 329 area ("She channels easy" callback)**
"She's a good one. You'd be a fool to lose her. Just so you know I know. Good." Four successive Haran beats producing warmth by announcing it. Haran says one thing obliquely and stops; repetition of the same sentiment collapses his register.

**2. Lines 358-369 ("The district breathed without him")**
The phrase appears twice within four sentences. "He stood with the small hard truth of it which was not a triumph" names the emotional register instead of showing it through the following clause.

**3. Lines 380-404 (Dorenne's letter as summary)**
Four "she said/the letter said" clauses render the letter as a memo. Carver inhabits the physical document and quotes the last paragraph verbatim, recovering Dorenne's bitter subtext. Hearthkeeper flattens her voice into institutional prose.

### Lyricist

**1. Lines 621-665 (trailing self-analysis after the spring mechanism)**
"The going on was the life, and the rest was the dressing of it." Direct aphoristic closure. No Trailing-Reframe Rule violation. The fear held beside the willingness should not resolve into a proverb.

**2. Lines 579-601 (Ryn's departure with counted footsteps)**
"He listened to the second step. He listened to the third." Two sentences doing the work of one. The additional clause explaining why the fourth is inaudible explains the listening instead of allowing it to stand.

**3. Lines 694-757 (closing walk over-populates with retrospective beats)**
The outline asks for the walk to pass the city as it is now. Lyricist names past events at each landmark ("the alley where in the autumn," "the corner where in the winter") which competes with the forward-facing weight of the chapter's close. These retrospective beats soften the closing image with nostalgia.
