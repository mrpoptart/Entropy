# Dorenne Kharren (Director of Infrastructure)

**Role:** Sixty. Director of the Infrastructure Office. Institutional-precise,
dry, calibrating, direct. Recognizes Ash's skill set on the council annex ramp
in Chapter 1 and offers him an aide position.

## Anchor line (verbatim in every prompt featuring Dorenne)

> Dorenne Kharren, a woman of about sixty with the bearing of someone
> accustomed to rooms rearranging themselves around her, sharp angular face,
> sharp gray eyes, iron-gray hair pulled back severely into a low knot,
> medium-tall and straight-backed, dressed in the formal half-cloak of a
> Council officeholder (dark charcoal fabric with the Infrastructure seal at
> the collar) over a high-collared dark coat and dark trousers, polished
> low boots.

## Detail

- **Build:** Medium-tall. Straight-backed. Carries authority physically;
  takes up the room she's in by standing fully upright in it.
- **Face:** Sharp, angular, lined. The kind of face that catalogs people.
  Expression by default: attentive, evaluating. Smile is dry, brief, rare.
- **Hair:** Iron-gray, pulled back severely into a low knot at the nape.
  "Exactly as long on it as she intended to and not a second more."
- **Eyes:** Gray, sharp, direct.
- **Hands:** Long-fingered, clean, capable. Often held clasped behind her
  back when listening, or palm-up in the Solathis gesture of offering an
  agreement.
- **Skin:** Pale, lined.
- **Posture:** Erect, square, unfussed. Takes the room.
- **Clothing (default):** Council officeholder's half-cloak (knee-length,
  dark charcoal wool) with the Infrastructure seal at the collar (a
  stylized stone/water motif). High-collared dark coat underneath,
  matching trousers, polished low boots. No jewelry.
- **Distinctive props:** Two or three younger staff trailing behind her
  in scenes where she walks through the annex. A leather portfolio under
  one arm.

## Negative prompt

- Not warm or grandmotherly; she is direct and institutional
- No bright colors or ornament
- No glasses
- Not hunched; she is straight-backed
- Smile is rare; do not render her smiling broadly
