# Maren (Ash's younger sister)

**Role:** The sister. Twenty-one. Structural surveyor / engineer-in-training.
Gifted channeler. Clean architecture: every line clean, every gesture
efficient. The composure could be mistaken for ease if you didn't know
it was built.

## Anchor line (verbatim in every prompt featuring Maren)

> Maren, Ash's younger sister at 21, slim and composed with the poised
> bearing of someone who has constructed every gesture, light brown wavy
> hair cut short and practical, sharp clear gray-green eyes, fair skin
> with a faint dusting of freckles across the nose, dressed in a fitted
> dark surveyor's coat over a high-collared tunic and practical trousers,
> a leather satchel slung across one shoulder.

## Detail

- **Build:** Slim, compact, athletic. Reads as efficient rather than
  delicate. Stands very still when listening.
- **Face:** Clean lines, structurally similar to Leska but younger and
  smoother. The kind of face that gives nothing away unless she lets it.
  Smile is bright when real, controlled when polite.
- **Hair:** Light brown, wavy, cut short (jaw-length at most). Practical,
  easy to pull back when working in the field.
- **Eyes:** Gray-green, sharp, very observant. Often described as
  "reading" a room or wall.
- **Hands:** Channeler's hands, finer-boned than her mother's. Often shown
  resting her palm flat on stone or wood, feeling for stress.
- **Skin:** Fair with a faint dusting of freckles across the nose and
  upper cheeks.
- **Posture:** Erect, composed. The "completed architecture" line is the
  load-bearing image. Every line clean.
- **Clothing (default):** Fitted dark-charcoal surveyor's coat (knee-length,
  belted), high-collared cream tunic underneath, dark practical trousers,
  ankle-high leather boots. Leather satchel across one shoulder containing
  notebooks, measuring tools.
- **Distinctive props:** Surveyor's satchel. A small graphite stick behind
  one ear when working. No jewelry.

## Negative prompt

- No long flowing hair
- No fancy dresses, no ornate clothing
- Not delicate, not waifish; she is composed and physically capable
- No glasses
- No bright colors; her palette is charcoal, cream, soft green
- Smile is restrained by default, not toothy
