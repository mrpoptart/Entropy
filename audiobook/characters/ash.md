# Ash (Ashlyn)

**Role:** Protagonist. Magically incapable in a city built around channeling.
At Book One start: 23, lives with parents, takes the stairs.

## Anchor line (verbatim in every prompt featuring Ash)

> Tall lanky 23-year-old working-class young man who reads 19 or 20, slim
> and long-limbed, wiry but underfed, slightly stooped and careful in
> posture. Dark brown shaggy wavy hair badly self-cut and overdue for a
> trim, deep-set hazel watchful eyes, warm habitual smile with crinkling
> eyes, slightly crooked nose, clean-shaven, sun-tanned skin. Visible
> capable calloused hands with small scars from blade slips and burns.
> Wearing threadbare autumn work layers: undyed linen shirt, faded dark
> blue wool overshirt with a mended elbow tear, patched quilted vest,
> heavily patched brown trousers, scuffed leather work boots. No jewelry
> or magical accessories. Grounded fantasy realism, humble, lived-in,
> warm but guarded.

## Art direction

Emphasize hands, posture, and clothing wear over beauty-shot heroism. Ash
should look useful, kind, tired, and careful, not heroic. Hands visible
whenever framing allows.

## Detail

- **Build:** Tall and lanky. Above average height with long limbs and a
  narrow frame. Slim and skinny rather than muscular. Wiry-strong from
  forty flights of stairs every day, but reads as underfed and stretched
  out, not bulky. Reads a few years younger than his actual 23, more like
  19 or 20.
- **Face:** Open, expressive when relaxed; armored with a habitual smile in
  public. Eyes that crinkle when he smiles. A slightly crooked nose (broken
  once as a child, never reset properly). Clean-shaven.
- **Hair:** Dark brown, wavy, shaggy, uncombed, clearly overdue for a cut.
  He cuts it himself, and badly, and not often enough.
- **Eyes:** Hazel, deep-set, watchful even when smiling.
- **Hands:** This is the load-bearing detail. **Capable hands.** Calloused,
  scars from blade slips and burns. Hands that have done things. They
  should be visible whenever framing allows.
- **Skin:** Sun-tanned, noticeably darker than his family's, the color of
  someone who walks outdoors year-round.
- **Posture:** Careful. He moves to be unobtrusive in crowded places, with
  precise foot placement. The body of someone who has learned not to take up
  space he hasn't earned. Slight roundness to the shoulders.
- **Clothing (default, autumn):** Working-class layers, visibly threadbare
  and lived-in. Inner shirt of undyed linen, outer shirt of faded dark blue
  wool with a small mended tear at one elbow, quilted vest with hand-stitched
  patches in mismatched fabric, much-patched heavy brown trousers, scuffed
  leather work boots with worn soles. Nothing new, nothing matched. No
  jewelry, no rings. No channeler accessories (no resonance stones, no
  pool-marker pendants).
- **Distinctive props:** A small leather notebook in an inner pocket. A
  multi-tool he made himself.

## Negative prompt

- No glasses, no beard, no stubble. Clean-shaven.
- Not blonde, not red-haired
- No glowing hands, no channeling glow around him (he can't channel)
- No formal/official clothing in early scenes
- Not heroic-posed; he is not a heroic figure yet

## Drift notes

- Post-Wellspring (later book one and book two), he carries new weight in
  the eyes. Tireder, more reserved. Mark with `--state post-wellspring`.
