# Visual Style Spec

This text is prepended to EVERY scene prompt. Keep it tight, declarative,
and consistent across the whole book.

## Style anchor (verbatim in every prompt)

> Photo-realistic cinematic film still. The aesthetic of a contemporary
> historical or fantasy prestige drama: natural soft daylight, warm earth
> tones, hand-built sets, authentic period-appropriate materials.
> Photo-real skin texture, fabric texture, stone and wood texture; visible
> detail in pores, hair strands, weave of cloth, grain of wood, dust and
> wear on clothing. Slightly desaturated color grade favoring warm tones
> (umber, ochre, dusty blue, weathered stone) with cool ambient shadows.
> Soft natural lighting (overcast daylight or warm late-afternoon golden
> hour), gentle shadows, not theatrical. Shallow depth of field on
> close-ups; deeper field on full-figure shots. Naturalistic posture and
> expression, candid framing, the feel of a still captured between camera
> takes rather than a posed portrait. Architectural and environmental
> detail: weathered stone walls, worn wooden doors, autumn leaves,
> hand-built lived-in surfaces. No illustration, no anime, no cel-shading,
> no painterly brushwork, no ink linework, no comic-book aesthetic,
> no glossy CGI, no plastic skin, no text or signatures.

## World style

- **City:** Solathis. A vertical stone city built on a high plateau, layered
  from gleaming upper districts (polished pale stone, seamless facades) to
  worn lower districts (cracked stonework, exposed mortar, weathered
  shutters). Mountains visible to the north (the Verenthi Range).
- **Magic visualization:** "Channeling" is a soft glow at the palm or in
  carved stone interfaces. Pale gold or warm-white light, never showy.
  Heating lattices are oval pale-stone panels. Transit lifts are stone
  platforms in shafts. Avoid sparks, lightning, glyphs, runes.
- **Period feel:** Pre-industrial-but-stable. No cars, no electricity, no
  gunpowder. Hand tools, oil lamps, layered fabric clothing. Think
  late-medieval Mediterranean meets terraced cliff city.
- **Light:** Cold autumn mornings outside, warm interior light from oil
  lamps and channeled stove heat. Strong contrast between the two.

## Composition rules

- 16:9 widescreen (1792x1024)
- One clear focal moment per image, drawn from the audio chunk's content
- Camera is often slightly low or eye-level; avoid drone-shot overhead views
- People rendered with weight and posture; bodies matter in this world
- Faces visible when characters are speaking; otherwise can be turned,
  shadowed, or distant
- No text, signage, or readable lettering anywhere in the image
