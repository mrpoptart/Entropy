# Leska (Ash's mother)

**Role:** The mother. Channels the household — stove, heating, water.
Carries fear quietly, names hard truths plainly, doesn't perform comfort.

## Anchor line (verbatim in every prompt featuring Leska)

> Leska, Ash's mother, a small wiry woman in her early fifties, not large
> but carrying herself with quiet authority, dark-brown hair streaked with
> gray pulled back in a practical knot, sharp-boned face with warm gray-blue
> eyes that miss nothing, weathered olive skin, capable channeler's hands,
> dressed in a long dark-blue work dress, a wrap-shawl, and a stained kitchen
> apron when at home.

## Detail

- **Build:** Small, wiry, compact. Not large. Stands with her shoulders
  squared. The "not a large woman, holds him like she was bracing a wall"
  description from chapter 1 is the load-bearing image.
- **Face:** Sharp-boned, lined at the eyes from squinting. Defaults to a
  composed, watchful expression. Smile is small but real.
- **Hair:** Dark brown streaked with gray, pulled back in a low knot or
  bun. Practical, never down.
- **Eyes:** Gray-blue, sharp, the kind of eyes that catalog detail.
- **Hands:** Channeler's hands. Fine-boned, capable. Often shown touching
  a heating lattice or stove interface with the palm flat.
- **Skin:** Olive, weathered from years of stove heat and outdoor errands.
- **Posture:** Square shoulders, quiet authority, chin slightly up. The
  "chin-lift" mentioned in her character spec: a small upward set of the
  chin when she will not back down.
- **Clothing (default, at home):** Long dark-blue work dress, sleeves
  pushed up, wrap-shawl over the shoulders, kitchen apron (stained,
  worn, beloved). Outside the house: a heavier woolen overdress and a
  long dark cloak.
- **Distinctive props:** Resonance stone (matched pair with Rendell's)
  on a cord around her neck. A wooden cooking spoon often in hand.

## Negative prompt

- No frilly or elaborate clothing
- No jewelry beyond the resonance stone
- Not a soft, pillowy maternal figure; she is wiry and direct
- No bright colors; her palette is dark blue, gray, brown, earth
- No glasses
