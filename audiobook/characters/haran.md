# Haran (the tinkerer / Ash's mentor)

**Role:** Old tinkerer. Runs a cluttered workshop where Ash spends afternoons.
Builds non-magical mechanisms (gears, springs, escapements). Quietly one of
the most powerful channelers alive, but lives like a pauper by choice.

## Anchor line (verbatim in every prompt featuring Haran)

> Haran, a wiry old man in his late sixties, lean and stooped slightly from
> decades at a workbench, weathered tanned hands stained with machine oil
> and graphite, close-cropped white hair, deep-set sharp brown eyes, a
> short white beard, dressed in a worn leather apron over a faded undyed
> linen shirt with sleeves rolled to the elbows, dark canvas trousers, and
> heavy leather work boots.

## Detail

- **Build:** Wiry, lean. Slight stoop from decades of leaning over a bench.
  Hands still steady.
- **Face:** Weather-lined, sharp-eyed. The kind of face that watches first
  and speaks later. Smile is rare and brief.
- **Hair:** White, close-cropped. Thinning on top but not bald.
- **Beard:** Short, white, neat. Trimmed close.
- **Eyes:** Brown, deep-set, very sharp. The "watches things work" eyes.
- **Hands:** Oil-stained, graphite-smudged. Fingers slightly knobbed at
  the joints from age but steady. The hands of a man who handles small
  components for hours.
- **Skin:** Tanned, weathered, lined.
- **Posture:** Slight stoop. Often shown leaning over a workbench, or
  sitting on a high stool with feet hooked on the rung.
- **Clothing (default):** Worn brown leather apron over a faded undyed
  linen shirt, sleeves rolled past the elbows, dark canvas trousers,
  heavy leather work boots. A small magnifying loupe on a cord around his
  neck.
- **Distinctive props:** The workshop itself — cluttered with tools,
  half-built mechanisms, jars of springs, lengths of brass tubing. A
  pipe (unlit) sometimes between his teeth when thinking.

## Negative prompt

- No wizard/sorcerer aesthetic. He does not look magical.
- No glowing hands (channeling, when shown, is restrained warm light at a
  fingertip — he rarely uses it on screen)
- No fancy clothing
- No fully bald
- Not robed; he is a working tradesman
