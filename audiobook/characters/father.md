# Rendell (Ash's father)

**Role:** Quiet patriarch. Craftsman. Hands-and-tools intelligence. Spent
twenty-three years quietly afraid Ash would never carry himself; never said
so.

## Anchor line (verbatim in every prompt featuring Rendell)

> Rendell, Ash's father, a broad-shouldered thick-handed man in his early
> fifties, built to carry things, with a craftsman's heavily calloused
> fingers and a faint scar on the left index finger from a chisel slip,
> close-cropped iron-gray hair, a short trimmed gray beard, weathered
> tanned skin, deep-set tired-warm eyes, a quiet steady bearing, work
> clothes of plain dark wool and a leather tool belt or apron.

## Detail

- **Build:** Broad-shouldered, thick-chested. Not tall — average height
  but reads as large because of frame. Thick wrists, heavy hands.
- **Face:** Weathered. Deep crow's feet from squinting at work in low
  light. A face that defaults to a quiet, steady expression. The kind of
  smile that's a small upward shift at one corner of the mouth.
- **Hair:** Iron-gray, close-cropped, no styling. Receding a little at
  the temples.
- **Beard:** Short, trimmed, gray with darker streaks. Not a long beard.
- **Eyes:** Brown, deep-set under heavy brows. Tired but warm.
- **Hands:** **Heavily calloused** with the specific pattern of a chisel-
  and-file worker: thick pads on the palms, roughness at the fingertips,
  scar on the left index finger from a chisel that slipped twenty years
  ago. Visible whenever framing allows.
- **Skin:** Tanned, weathered, lined.
- **Posture:** Steady, grounded, low center of gravity. The body of a man
  who lifts and carries for a living.
- **Clothing (default):** Coarse dark-brown wool shirt, leather apron or
  belt with hand tools (chisels, files, a small mallet), heavy canvas
  trousers, well-broken work boots. Coat hung by the door when home.
- **Distinctive props:** Leather case of files and chisels. Resonance
  stone (small palm-sized smooth stone) on a cord at his belt for messages
  from Leska.

## Negative prompt

- No clean polished office look; he is a working man
- No glasses
- Not bald; receding but still has hair
- No flashy clothing, no jewelry beyond the resonance stone cord
- No channeling glow on his hands; he reinforces structure but it's quiet,
  not showy. Channeling, when shown, is faint warm light at the fingertip.
